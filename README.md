## Why this list?
Most popular internship lists focus on the US. Students in Asia end up checking dozens of career pages, LinkedIn, and group chats. This repo puts HK, SG and KR roles in one place and **refreshes them every day straight from company job boards**, so closed roles drop off on their own.

Visa eligibility, deadlines and required languages are rarely stated on job boards, so they are **not guessed**: if you know them for a posting, [add them](CONTRIBUTING.md) and a badge will show up.
